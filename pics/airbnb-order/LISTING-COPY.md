## Full description

**The space**
A warm, wood-trimmed 2-bedroom condo inside Everline Resort & Spa (formerly Resort at
Squaw Creek) in Olympic Valley. Sleeps 4, with 1 full bath. Floor-to-ceiling corner
windows pull in the mountain and meadow views; a stone gas fireplace makes it the place
you want to come back to after a day on the snow. A king bed in the bedroom plus a pullout
sofa bed sleep four. A kitchenette (microwave,
dishwasher, fridge, toaster, cookware, and cooking basics) handles easy mornings and
après snacks, plus in-unit laundry access, a desk/workspace, and fast WiFi.

**Ski-in / ski-out**
Direct access to Palisades Tahoe — home of the 1960 Winter Olympics — via the Resort
Chair. Ski valet, sports shop, and a winter ice rink are all on property.

**Resort amenities** (shared with all resort guests, included with your stay)
- Two heated outdoor pools + hot tubs, open year-round
- Full spa and 24-hour fitness center with sauna
- Five on-site dining outlets
- Summer: 18-hole championship golf, tennis, 6+ miles of Nordic trails, 150-ft waterslide
- Free resort shuttle
- Free gym access

**Great for groups**
A second unit right next door can be booked alongside this one — ideal for larger families
or friends traveling together. Message before booking to arrange both.

**Location**
Base of Palisades Tahoe, Olympic Valley — ~20 min to Lake Tahoe's north shore, ~45 min
to Reno-Tahoe airport.
